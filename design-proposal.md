# Design Proposal: Marina Batlló Rius Portfolio

## Brand Narrative
**"Human-centered data & AI applied to high-impact business and health contexts"**

You bridge three worlds that rarely meet: rigorous data science, human-centered design thinking, and real business impact. Your superpower is making complex technology usable, scalable, and meaningful—whether that's accelerating drug discovery pipelines or building AI tools that people actually want to use.

---

## Direction A: "Lumina" — Warm Tech, Human Glow

### Mood
Airy, approachable, sophisticated. Like morning light in a modern lab—clean but warm. This direction emphasizes the human side of your work while maintaining technical credibility.

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors**
- Background Primary: `#FDFBF7` (warm off-white, like fine paper)
- Background Secondary: `#F5F0E8` (soft cream)
- Text Primary: `#1A1A1A` (near-black)
- Text Secondary: `#6B6B6B` (warm gray)

**Accent Colors**
- Accent Primary: `#D4A574` (warm sand/amber—confident but understated)
- Accent Secondary: `#8B9A7C` (sage green—health/life sciences nod)
- Accent Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #D4A574 0%, #C9956B 100%)`

**Dark Mode**
- Background Primary: `#0F0F0F`
- Background Secondary: `#1A1A1A`
- Text Primary: `#F5F0E8`
- Accent Primary: `#E8C9A0` (lighter amber for dark mode)

### Typography

**Headlines**: `Sora` (geometric sans, modern but warm)
- Weights: 300, 400, 500, 600
- Large headlines: 300 weight for elegance

**Body**: `Inter` (highly readable, professional)
- Weights: 400, 500, 600

**Mono/Labels**: `JetBrains Mono` (technical credibility)
- Weight: 400

### Visual Elements
- Subtle grain texture overlay (2% opacity)
- Soft shadows: `0 4px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`
- Border radius: 12px for cards, 8px for buttons
- Thin borders: `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`

### Signature Visual
Animated flowing lines that suggest data streams and neural pathways—organic yet precise. Soft amber glow on hover.

## Direction B: "Prism" — Cool Precision, Clarity Focus

### Mood
Crisp, confident, editorial. Like a premium science journal meets Apple design. This direction emphasizes analytical rigor and precision.

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors**
- Background Primary: `#FAFAFA` (pure white-gray)
- Background Secondary: `#F2F2F2` (light gray)
- Text Primary: `#0A0A0A` (true black)
- Text Secondary: `#737373` (neutral gray)

**Accent Colors**
- Accent Primary: `#2563EB` (cobalt blue—trust, intelligence)
- Accent Secondary: `#0891B2` (cyan—tech, innovation)
- Accent Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #2563EB 0%, #0891B2 100%)`

**Dark Mode**
- Background Primary: `#0A0A0A`
- Background Secondary: `#141414`
- Text Primary: `#FAFAFA`
- Accent Primary: `#3B82F6` (brighter blue for dark mode)

### Typography

**Headlines**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (modern, slightly geometric, confident)
- Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700

**Body**: `Inter` (consistent with Direction A)
- Weights: 400, 500, 600

**Mono/Labels**: `SF Mono` or `JetBrains Mono`
- Weight: 400

### Visual Elements
- Clean, minimal shadows: `0 2px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`
- Border radius: 16px for cards (softer), 12px for buttons
- Subtle grid pattern background (very light)

### Signature Visual
Geometric shapes that morph and connect—circles, lines, nodes—representing data networks and systems thinking. Blue glow on interaction.

## Recommendation

**I recommend Direction A: "Lumina"** with **Headline Option 1**.

**Rationale**:
1. The warm palette differentiates you from the sea of blue-tech portfolios
2. It better communicates your human-centered approach—a key differentiator
3. The sand/amber + sage combination subtly nods to health/life sciences without being literal
4. It feels more "you"—approachable, thoughtful, intentional
5. The warmth balances the technical credibility you already have

The "Lumina" direction creates a premium, memorable brand that stands out while maintaining complete professionalism.
